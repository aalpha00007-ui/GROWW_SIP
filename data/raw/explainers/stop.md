---
key: stop
title: What stopping a SIP does
status: approved
---
Stopping a SIP ends the instalments that have not been paid yet. It does not refund any money and does not sell any units. The units you already bought stay invested in the fund, and their value keeps moving with the fund's NAV until you choose to redeem them. Redeeming is a separate step. To invest again later, you start a new SIP. If you only need a break, you can skip the next instalment or pause for 1 to 3 instalments instead.
